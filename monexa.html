What Monexa is

Monexa is a shared expense and income tracker (formerly GastoControl). You can log movements, categories, budgets, subscriptions and goals in a group with other people.

Data we process

  • Account: email and authentication (Firebase Auth).
  • App data: expenses/incomes, categories, notes, budgets, subscriptions, goals and group membership in Firestore.
  • Optional: receipt images (OCR) and microphone (voice expenses), only if you use those features.

Where data is stored

Account and group data are stored in Google Firebase (Flow Home Apps project). Local preferences may stay on the device (e.g. active group).

Permissions (Android)

  • Internet: sync with Firebase and online features.
  • Camera / photos: scan or attach receipts (optional).
  • Microphone: voice expense entry (optional).

Third parties

We do not sell your data. We use providers needed to run the app (Firebase/Google). If you enable third-party OCR (e.g. OCR.space), the receipt image may be sent to that service under its policy.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion at soporte.flowhomeapps@gmail.com. You can also delete data in-app or by uninstalling (local data).
