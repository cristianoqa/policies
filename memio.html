What Memio is

Memio is an Android meme generator. You add text to images on the device. No account or sign-in is required.

Data that stays on the device

  • Photos you pick as templates and memes you create are processed locally.
  • A short internal list of recent memes (app storage).
  • If you save a meme, it may be copied to your gallery with the permission you grant.

What we do not do

  • We do not create a cloud account or sync your images to a Memio server.
  • We do not sell data or use invasive ads or third-party advertising trackers.

App permissions (Android)

  • Photos / gallery: pick your own image and, if you ask, save the meme.
  • Share: uses the system sheet (WhatsApp, Drive, etc.); you choose the destination.

Your rights

You can delete memes in the app, in the gallery, or by uninstalling Memio (local app data is removed). Questions: soporte.flowhomeapps@gmail.com.

Changes

We will publish updates in the app and, where required, at the policy URL listed on Google Play.
