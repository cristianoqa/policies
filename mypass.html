What MyPass is

MyPass is a password vault encrypted on your device. Your master password is not sent to us in readable form. There is no remote email/SMS reset of the master password; recovery on-device uses your Recovery Key (if you saved it) plus encrypted backups. Cloud copy (Supabase, if you enable it) is optional and uploads already encrypted data only.

Data that stays on the device

  • Credentials and master password in memory only while you use the app.
  • Encrypted vault and preferences (auto-lock, language, etc.) in platform secure storage.
  • Biometrics: the OS handles fingerprint/face; MyPass does not store biometric images.

OS backups: the app disables automatic backup of its data where supported, to reduce leakage. Uninstalling or â€œDelete vaultâ€ removes MyPass data on that device.

If you enable cloud copy

  • Account: session identity (email or OAuth per your Supabase project).
  • What is uploaded: encrypted vault blob and revision metadata (timestamps). Your master password and readable passwords are not sent.
  • Backend operator: you or whoever runs the Supabase project linked to the build.

App permissions (Android)

  • Internet: only when you use cloud sync or OAuth.
  • Biometrics: optional unlock on the device.
