What Lunera is

Lunera is a menstrual health app for Android/iOS. It logs symptoms, predicts cycle phases, and can export optional reports.

Data we process

  • Daily logs (bleeding, pain, mood, symptoms, notes) are stored locally on your device.
  • Guest mode creates no account and does not send symptoms to servers.
  • If you enable sync later, health data is encrypted on-device before upload.
  • We do not sell data or show invasive ads.

Permissions

Notifications (optional reminders), biometrics (optional lock), storage (backup/PDF).

Purchases
