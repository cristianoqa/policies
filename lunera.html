What Lunera is

Lunera is a FemTech menstrual health app. It predicts cycle phases, supports daily symptom logging, a flexible calendar, an educational encyclopedia and local AI assistance. Health data is handled with a local-first approach.

Data on the device

  • Daily logs, cycles, settings and preferences (language, theme, icons).
  • Guest mode does not create a cloud account.
  • Biometrics (optional): the OS manages Face ID/fingerprint; Lunera does not store biometric images.
  • Educational AI uses your cycle context on device for local replies.

If you enable account / sync

  • Email is hashed or encrypted locally depending on the flow.
  • Optional backup: encrypted blob (ciphertext). We do not send symptoms in clear text.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional reminders).
  • Biometrics (optional lock).
  • Storage (backup / PDF).

Purchases

Lunera Pro is handled by the stores (Google Play / App Store). Ko-fi/PayPal are voluntary support and do not unlock digital features.

Your rights

You can delete data by uninstalling the app or restoring/exporting backups. Questions: soporte.flowhomeapps@gmail.com.
