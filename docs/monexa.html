Terms of service

What Monexa is

Shared expense tracker: movements, categories, budgets, subscriptions and goals in a group.

Data

  • Account via Firebase Auth; group data in Firestore; optional ticket OCR and voice input.

Data controller

Flow Home Apps (independent app project). Privacy contact: soporte.flowhomeapps@gmail.com. Tax ID or further controller details can be requested by email when exercising your rights.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for camera, mic, third-party OCR.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Retention

While account/group exist; delete movements in-app or request account deletion.

Recipients and processors

Google Firebase; optional OCR providers; your group members see group data.

We do not sell personal data or share it for third-party advertising.

International transfers

Firebase/Google may process outside the EEA under their safeguards.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and, where applicable, portability at soporte.flowhomeapps@gmail.com. We respond within legal deadlines.

You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in Spain: AEPD).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. If we learn we hold such data without a valid basis, we will delete it.

Security

We apply reasonable measures (encryption in transit when online, OS-protected local storage, and end-to-end encryption where stated). No system is 100% secure.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date reflects changes. Material updates will also be reflected in the store listing when applicable.

Notice
