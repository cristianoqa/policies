Summary

Misiva (formerly MensajeTexto) is an Android app that generates messages with Google Gemini and sends them via WhatsApp according to schedules you define. Data is processed and stored on your device. We do not run our own servers that receive your messages.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not read or store third-party WhatsApp conversations.
  • We do not sell or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not upload your messages to our servers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or ads.

Third-party services

  • Google Gemini API: if you set an API key, prompts are sent to Google to generate text. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): the app opens WhatsApp on your device. Misiva does not access Meta’s servers.

Accessibility service

Misiva uses an AccessibilityService only to automate sending in WhatsApp (find and tap Send). It:

  • Only acts when a scheduled send is due or you confirm from a notification.
  • Does not read unrelated screens for other purposes.
  • Does not transmit other apps’ content to external servers.

You must enable the service manually in Settings → Accessibility.

Retention and deletion

  • You can clear history and delete scheduled sends and the API key in the app.
  • Deferred sends expire automatically (limited window).
  • Uninstalling removes all local data.

Backups

allowBackup=false: app data is not included in Google backups by default.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will publish updates before relevant releases. The date above will change.
