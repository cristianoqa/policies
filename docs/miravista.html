Summary

Miravista mirrors your screen (and optional playback audio) on your local Wi‑Fi only. No cloud mirroring servers.

Data

Data controller

Flow Home Apps (independent app project). Privacy contact: soporte.flowhomeapps@gmail.com. Tax ID or further controller details can be requested by email when exercising your rights.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Contract/service (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for MediaProjection capture.

Retention

Real-time stream only; no cloud storage of captures by Miravista.

Recipients and processors

Only LAN devices you share URL+PIN with.

We do not sell personal data or share it for third-party advertising.

International transfers

No international transfers in intended use (stays on LAN).

Your rights

The app is not directed at children under 13. If we learn we hold such data without a valid basis, we will delete it.

Changes
