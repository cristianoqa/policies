Summary

Miravista streams your screen (and optional app playback audio) only on your local Wi‑Fi. There is no cloud mirroring server and we do not sell personal data.

Data we use

DataPurposeWhere
Screen contentMJPEG stream to the TV browserDevice + LAN
Playback audio (optional)PCM via AudioPlaybackCaptureWith permission + MediaProjection
Local IPURL and QR codeOn-device only
Session PINAuthenticate the local streamGenerated on-device

What we do not do

  • We do not upload capture or audio to Miravista servers.
  • No accounts or third-party analytics in this version.
  • No precise location.
  • No overlay over other apps.

Local network

The embedded HTTP server listens on port 8080. Anyone with the URL including the PIN can view the stream. Stop mirroring when finished. Do not share the URL outside your home network.

DRM

Some protected apps may not send video or audio to the mirror. That is an Android system limitation.
