What Lunera is

Lunera is a FemTech menstrual health app (native Android plus an educational web PWA). It predicts cycle phases, supports daily symptom logging, a calendar, life stages (contraception, pregnancy/postpartum, menopause), an encyclopedia and local AI assistance. Health data is handled with a local-first approach.

Data on the device

  • Daily logs, cycles, settings and preferences (language, theme, icons, life stage).
  • Guest mode does not create a cloud account.
  • Biometrics (optional): the OS manages Face ID/fingerprint; Lunera does not store biometric images.
  • Educational AI uses your context on device for local replies. It does not diagnose or prescribe.

PWA (web)

The web version at cristianoqa.github.io/lunera stores minimal data (last period, cycle length) in the browser’s localStorage on this device. It is not sent to a Lunera server. You can delete it by clearing site data.

If you enable account / sync

  • Email is hashed or encrypted locally depending on the flow.
  • Optional backup: encrypted blob (ciphertext). We do not send symptoms in clear text.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional reminders).
  • Biometrics (optional lock).
  • Storage (backup / PDF).

Purchases

Lunera Pro is handled by the stores (Google Play / App Store). Ko-fi/PayPal are voluntary support and do not unlock digital features.

Your rights

You can delete data by uninstalling the app, restoring/exporting backups, or, on the PWA, clearing site storage. Questions: soporte.flowhomeapps@gmail.com.

Changes

We will publish updates in the app and, where required, at the policy URL listed on Google Play.

Legal notice

This text describes how the app works technically; it is not a substitute for personal legal or medical advice. Lunera is educational and is not a contraceptive method.
