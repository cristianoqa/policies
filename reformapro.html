What ReformaPRO is

ReformaPRO is an app to create and manage renovation quotes from a phone: clients, catalog, line items, taxes, margins, PDF and client delivery.

Data we process

  • Account: login email and authentication.
  • Business data: clients, catalog items, quotes, templates and generated documents.
  • Contact data: client name, phone, email and address if you enter them.

Where data is stored

App data is stored in cloud infrastructure used by Flow Home Apps to operate ReformaPRO. Temporary preferences may also remain on the device.

Permissions (Android)

  • Internet: sign in, sync data and generate documents.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data and we do not show invasive advertising inside the app.
